A live view of our objectives and the key results that inform them. Status on each objective is a judgment call by the team. KRs are the instruments — we can add, edit, or retire them as context changes. Click any status, note, or next step to update it directly.

Why we use OKRs

Objectives are the commitments we hold to each other. Key Results are how we'll know we got there. The commitment is durable; the measurements are instruments we refine.

How we use this dashboard

A learning tool, not an evaluation tool. Two reads, objective and KRs, let us see where the numbers say one thing and the reality says another. The gap is the conversation.

What you should do

Before each review, open this page. Update the status on your objective, note what's driving it, name the next move. If something's off track or you see a problem coming, raise a flag. Top flags shape the agenda.
