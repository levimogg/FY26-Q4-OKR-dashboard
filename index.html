Why we use OKRs

Objectives and Key Results force us to be specific about what we're trying to accomplish (the objective) and how we'll know we got there (the key results). They turn strategy into measurable commitments so the whole team can see what matters and where we're investing our energy.

How we use this dashboard

This is a learning tool, not an evaluation tool. We're not grading people — we're tracking where the work is moving, where it's stuck, and what we need to adjust. The goal is to surface the right conversations early so we can shift resources and thinking toward what matters most.

What you should do

Before each monthly review, open this page. Look at your function's OKRs and the org-wide ones you care about. If something is off track or you see a challenge coming, raise a flag. The top flags shape the agenda. This is how we learn out loud together.
