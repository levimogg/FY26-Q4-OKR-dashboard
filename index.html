A live view of our objectives and the key results that inform them. Objective status is a judgment call by the team. Key results are the instruments; add, edit, or retire them in the sheet as context changes. Click any status, progress bar, note, or next step to update it directly.

Why we use OKRs

Objectives are the commitments we hold to each other. Key Results are how we'll know we got there. The commitment is durable. The measurements are instruments we refine as we learn.

How we use this dashboard

A learning tool, not an evaluation tool. Two reads, objective and KRs, let us see where the numbers say one thing and reality says another. The gap is the conversation.

What you should do

Before each review, open this page. Update the status on your objective, name what's driving it, and say the next move. If something is off track, raise a flag. The team works on what you surface.

How to use this

  1. Find a card you're responsible for.
  2. Click the pill to mark it on track, at risk, or off track.
  3. Open the key results underneath. Click the bar to update progress.
  4. Add a note on why, and name the next move.
  5. Flag anything the team needs to weigh in on.
